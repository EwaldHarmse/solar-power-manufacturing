# Solar Power Manufacturing: Business Analysis

Business Analysis 2.2 project modelling **SunCore Energy Solutions**, a South African company that manufactures, supplies and installs solar panels, inverters and battery storage. The project maps the company's core business processes with the REA (Resources, Events, Agents) model and designs the database tables that support them.

- Full report: [`Solar Power Manufacturing.docx`](Solar%20Power%20Manufacturing.docx)
- Integrated REA diagram: [`BA THEORY.drawio.png`](BA%20THEORY.drawio.png)

![Integrated REA diagram](BA%20THEORY.drawio.png)

## Contents

1. **Business background**: products and services, target market, short/medium/long-term goals, information needs and integration problems
2. **Business processes**: value chain, sales and collection, acquisition and payment, conversion (manufacturing), and how they integrate
3. **REA diagrams**: one per process plus an integrated REA model, with integration problems and solutions
4. **Reports** the system should produce
5. **Example tables with data**: manufacturing supervisor, inventory clerk, material requisition, raw materials, material issuance, machine and labour operations, production runs, finished goods and production orders
